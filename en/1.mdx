# Welcome to AcadChain

AcadChain is a revolutionary blockchain-based platform designed to streamline and secure academic credential verification. Our system ensures the authenticity and integrity of academic records while providing a seamless experience for educational institutions and employers.

## Key Features

- **Secure Credential Verification**: Instant verification of academic credentials through blockchain technology
- **Decentralized Storage**: Academic records stored securely on the blockchain
- **Easy Integration**: Simple API integration for educational institutions
- **Global Accessibility**: Access verified credentials from anywhere in the world

## Getting Started

To get started with AcadChain, follow these steps:

1. Set up your development environment
2. Configure your institution's credentials
3. Start issuing verified academic records

For detailed instructions, please refer to our [Deployment Guide](/en/deployment) and [Configuration Guide](/en/configuration).
